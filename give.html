Give

Thank you for your generosity

There are many ways to support God's work here. We don't expect you to give — but if you'd like to, thank you.

Ways to give

However you'd like to give

A box at the back of church is always available, or you can use one of the methods below. However you give, and whatever the amount, it makes a real difference.

The most helpful way to give

Bank transfer or standing order

Giving straight from your bank is the simplest, most direct way — your gift reaches the church in full. Setting up a standing order for regular giving is a wonderful help: it's easy to arrange with your bank and gives us a steady, predictable income to plan around.

Donate with JustGiving

By text

Quick for a small gift. Text TBC followed by an amount to 70085 — anything from £1 to £30. For example, TBC 5 gives £5, or TBC 20 gives £20.

Texts cost your donation amount plus one standard rate message.

Gift Aid it — free extra income for the church

If you're a UK taxpayer, Gift Aid lets us claim an extra 25p for every £1 you give, at no cost to you. Just ask for a Gift Aid form in church, or mention it when you set up your giving.
