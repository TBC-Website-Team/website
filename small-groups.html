Small Groups

Small groups are where church life happens midweek — a handful of people meeting in homes, the church café, or online to read the Bible, pray, and support each other. There's a group for all sorts of people and times. Find one that fits, or email smallgroups@thatchambaptist.org.uk and we'll help you find the right one.
