Here for life's moments

Mark life's significant moments with us

From welcoming a new child to celebrating a marriage or remembering someone dear, we'd be honoured to support you and your family.

A church for every season

You don't have to mark life's milestones alone

At Thatcham Baptist Church, we want to walk alongside people in our community through moments of joy, commitment and loss.

Whether you're making plans or simply exploring what's possible, we'll listen and help you work out what feels right for you.

Birth · Marriage · Death

How we can be there for you

Each service is personal. We'll take time to understand what matters to you and your family.

Birth

Thanksgiving & dedication

Would you like to bring your baby or child for a service of thanksgiving and dedication as part of a Sunday service? We can tailor the occasion to make it meaningful for you and your family.

Marriage

A day to celebrate

We are registered for weddings and have our own registrars available. If you'd like, our Pastor, David can also offer you a couple of marriage preparation sessions as you get ready for married life.

Death

A service to remember

Together with your funeral director, we'll help you through the process of planning a funeral service. Our facilities and easy parking also make it straightforward for family and friends to stay for a reception afterwards.

Start a conversation

We're here to listen

If you'd like to talk about a dedication, wedding or funeral, please get in touch with the church office. We'll help you explore the next steps with care and without pressure.
